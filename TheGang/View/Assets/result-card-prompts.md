# Cartes de résultat

Sprites créés avec l'outil ImageGen intégré. Le jeu cadre les illustrations dans les cartes WPF à l'aide de `CroppedBitmap`.

## Carte d'or (`result-gold.png`)

Génération initiale :

> Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: front face of one landscape result card for a cooperative poker heist desktop game, to be legible both as a small scoreboard sprite and enlarged as a result splash. Single horizontal playing-card-like rectangular object centered on a genuinely transparent canvas. Aspect ratio of the physical card approximately 3:2. Ivory edge and subtle rounded corners; rich bright golden yellow interior with refined black and antique gold art deco lines. At the center, an unmistakable small open bank vault or treasure bag overflowing with gold bars and coins, bold simple silhouette and polished 2D board-game illustration, warm celebratory gleam. Inspired by premium tabletop heist game components, no brand copy. Keep the entire card visible with even transparent margins. No floor, hands, background, other cards, text, letters, numbers, logos or watermark. Make the gold symbol clear at thumbnail size.

Édition finale pour réserver le coffre ouvert à la troisième réussite :

> Use case: precise-object-edit. Asset type: landscape result card sprite for a cooperative poker heist desktop game. Edit the provided golden art deco card. Preserve exactly the rectangular golden yellow card layout, ivory rounded edge, black/gold geometric border, perspective, lighting, and all details outside the center illustration. Replace ONLY the central OPEN VAULT and its door with a compact tied black-and-gold loot bag visibly brimming with gold coins, plus a small heap of loose gold coins around it. The result represents ONE successful heist and must NOT depict an opened safe, a vault door, a treasure chest, or a completed three-heist victory. Bold simple silhouette readable at 45-pixel thumbnail size, premium 2D board-game illustration. No text, letters, numbers, logos, people, watermark, or extra cards. Keep the same full card framing and image dimensions.

## Carte d'alarme (`result-alarm.png`)

> Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: front face of one landscape result card for a cooperative poker heist desktop game, legible both as a small scoreboard sprite and enlarged as a result splash. Single horizontal playing-card-like rectangle, same approximate framing and 3:2 aspect ratio as a premium art deco heist game's gold result card. Ivory edge with subtle rounded corners; charcoal black interior with deep burgundy enamel and fine antique gold geometric lines. Centerpiece is one large vivid RED SECURITY ALARM SIREN BEACON with radiating sharp red light streaks and lens sparkle, unmistakably signaling a failed heist. Dramatic polished 2D board-game illustration, strong readable silhouette at thumbnail size. Entire card fully visible, isolated with even genuine transparent alpha margins outside its edges. No floor, hands, background gradient, other cards, text, letters, numbers, logos, watermark, no vault door. Red alarm is the focal point.
