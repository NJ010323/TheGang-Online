# Sprites du coffre

Créés avec l'outil ImageGen intégré. Les fichiers PNG ont un fond transparent et sont embarqués dans l'application WPF.

## Coffre fermé (`vault-closed.png`)

> Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: transparent PNG game sprite for a cooperative poker heist desktop game. A single massive antique bank vault, straight-on frontal view, centered, fully closed and visibly locked. Circular reinforced door nested in a square blackened steel frame, brass and antique gold art deco filigree, subtle burgundy red enamel details, coherent with premium illustrated board-game cards. Heavy round central lock and visible bolts. Dramatic painterly 2D game illustration, crisp readable silhouette at medium size, polished material highlights. The whole vault including frame is fully visible with ample transparent margins on all sides. Absolutely no background, floor, cast shadow outside silhouette, scenery, text, letters, numbers, logo, watermark, humans, or cards. Genuine transparent alpha background.

## Coffre ouvert (`vault-open.png`)

Édition du sprite fermé comme image de référence.

> Use case: precise-object-edit. Asset type: second transparent PNG game sprite for an animated heist result. Edit the provided closed vault image into its OPEN state, as the same object seen from exactly the same frontal camera angle at identical scale, crop, square outer frame, blackened steel, antique gold and burgundy art deco details. Preserve the outer frame and its position. Swing the massive circular vault door open on its right hinges toward the viewer, with a clearly visible dark chamber inside FULL of luminous gold bars and gold coins, bright warm golden light spilling from it. The heavy circular door remains visibly attached to the right side, not floating. Keep the whole object fully visible inside generous transparent margins. This image will be crossfaded over the closed state, so preserve visual alignment and material style. No other changes. No background, floor, scenery, text, logos, watermark, people, cards. Genuine transparent alpha background.
